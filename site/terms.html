Using Prism

Last updated November 9, 2026

Prism is a Zoom app that answers a meeting participant's questions about the screen being shared. It is a student project in development, built by Richitha Rekula during the Next Lab Zoom Fellowship at Arizona State University. It is not a product of Zoom or of Arizona State University, and neither has reviewed or endorsed it. Prism was called ZoomLens until November 2026. By adding or using Prism you agree to these terms. If you don't agree, don't use it.

What Prism is, and isn't

  • Prism is provided free of charge, as it is, while in development. Features may change, stop working, or be removed, and the service may be unavailable at times.
  • Answers are produced by an AI. They can be incomplete, misread the screen, or be wrong. Don't rely on Prism for decisions where a mistake matters, such as medical, legal, financial or safety decisions, without checking what's actually on screen.
  • The accessibility features, Announce changes and Color Assist, are meant to help, but they have not yet been tested with the people they are designed for and are not a replacement for an accessible presentation.

Using it responsibly

  • Use Prism only in meetings where you're allowed to, and follow your organization's rules about apps that process meeting content.
  • Prism sends pictures of the shared screen to an AI provider to answer you, as described in the Privacy Policy. Don't start it in a meeting whose shared content must not leave the meeting.
  • Don't use Prism to harass anyone, to break the law, or to get around a meeting's or organization's restrictions on recording or sharing content.
  • Don't try to read other participants' answers, overload the service, or interfere with how it works. Each person can ask a limited number of questions, so it stays affordable to run.

Zoom

Prism runs inside Zoom, so Zoom's own terms and settings apply too. A meeting host decides whether Prism may start in their meeting.

Your content

Prism claims no ownership of meeting content, your questions or its answers. It uses them only to answer you, and keeps them only for the short times the Privacy Policy lists.

Changes and contact
