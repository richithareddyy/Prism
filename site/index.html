What's in Prism today, and what's a concept

In Prism today

  • Describe screen, Explain, and What changed?
  • Typed questions, and follow-ups that build on the last answer
  • Announce changes and Color Assist
  • Answers only you see, without the presenter being told
  • A Start button in the panel, with the host's approval when a participant presses it
  • A switch for the host to turn Prism off for everyone
  • Your last answer kept for 30 minutes, so follow-ups survive reopening the panel

Concepts shown here

  • Selecting part of the screen, or drawing a box around it
  • Answers that link back to the screen
  • Suggested questions
  • A notice when the screen changes; the panel describes the change instead when Announce changes is on

Prism only watches for changes while Announce changes or Color Assist is on. To notice a change it keeps a 64 by 36 grey thumbnail of the last screen it described, not a recording.

♿ Accessibility

Descriptions and explanations alongside your meeting.

Prism helps when details are visual or depend on color. Turn on these optional modes directly inside your private panel.

👁️ Blind & Low Vision

  • Announce Changes: Prism automatically detects meaningful screen changes and generates AI descriptions.
  • Screen Reader Support: Descriptions appear in a live text region designed for your screen reader to announce out loud.

🎨 Color Blindness

  • Color Assist: Guides AI answers to use clear labels and values instead of relying on color alone.
  • Explain Colors: Color Assist flags potentially confusing colors. Choose Explain colors to ask what they mean in a chart or spreadsheet.

🧪 Help shape the accessibility beta

These features are implemented and in active development. We need your help. They have not yet been validated with blind, low-vision, or color-blind users. If that's you, your feedback can help shape our testing and improvements.

Privacy

Your answers reach only you.

Protected

  • Answers go to the person who asked. The server sends each answer to exactly one person.
  • Others never see it. Your questions and answers do not appear in the meeting, for other participants or the presenter.
  • Who's asking is checked with Zoom. Zoom confirms who each person is, so nobody can pretend to be you and read your answers.
  • Your questions aren't logged. The server notes that a question was asked, never what it said.

How Prism handles meeting content

Questions

What people ask first.

Can I use it in my own meetings?

Not yet. Prism is in development and runs on a test Zoom account. The source is on GitHub.

Does the presenter know I'm asking?

No. Prism does not notify the presenter, and your questions and answers are sent only to you. Whether presenters should be told is the main open question for ethical review.

Can other people in the meeting see my questions?

No. Every message the server sends goes to one person, and there is no way to send to everyone. This is tested with ten participants asking at the same moment.

What gets sent to the AI model?

One still image of the shared screen and your question. They go to a third-party vision model to be answered, and the image is not filtered first. With Announce changes or Color Assist on, a still is also sent when the screen changes in a way worth describing, at most once every 10 seconds; for Color Assist, only when its own analysis finds colors that could be confused.

Is the meeting recorded?

No. Only the last few seconds of the screen share are kept, in memory, so an image is ready when you ask. Prism doesn't receive the meeting's audio or transcript at all. Nothing is kept on disk unless a developer turns that on for testing, and anything saved that way is deleted after 24 hours. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

Does it work with a screen reader?

Prism includes a live region for new answers, labeled accessibility switches, and keyboard controls. Announce changes adds descriptions when a meaningful screen change is detected. These are implemented features; the experience has not yet been validated with blind or low-vision users. See our accessibility beta and feedback options.

Can it help if I'm color blind?

Color Assist is designed to help: it asks the AI to use labels and values instead of color alone and offers color explanations. Simulations flag colors that may be confused; they do not represent every person's vision. Validation with color-blind users is still ahead. See our accessibility beta and feedback options.

Is there a limit on questions?

Yes. Each question is a paid call to the model, so each person can ask once every few seconds and up to a daily limit. The panel says how long to wait.
